My research lies in machine learning and computer vision, with a focus on human gait recognition, medical image analysis and sign language recognition.

I am an Assistant Professor (Selection Grade) in the Department of Computer Science at Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science, Chennai. I teach undergraduate and postgraduate students and guide their academic projects.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Kannur University as a DST INSPIRE Fellow, where my doctoral work on human gait recognition received a Best Paper Award. I have published in SCI-indexed journals including Multimedia Tools and Applications and Pattern Recognition and Image Analysis, and I serve as a reviewer for Multimedia Tools and Applications (Springer) and the International Journal of Image and Graphics.

I welcome collaboration on gait biometrics, medical image analysis and applied deep learning, and I am happy to hear from prospective students interested in these areas.
